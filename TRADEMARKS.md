The name is not part of that. The name Konzilo and Konzilo's logos are trademarks of Kntnt Sweden AB, of Gothenburg, Sweden, the company that develops Konzilo and is called Kntnt here. The license grants no right to them (its section 7(e)), and neither does the site exception ([`SITE-EXCEPTION.md`](SITE-EXCEPTION.md)). This policy says how they may be used.

## What the name is for

The name tells people what they get. Whoever meets Konzilo, as software or as a service, should get what Kntnt has released under that name, working as it does there. A name that anyone could put on anything would stop saying that, and one bad service would be held against every good one.

So the code is free for all, and the name is for what Kntnt releases and for those who answer for how they use it.

## What needs no permission

- **Saying what is true.** That a site is built with Konzilo, that a product works with it, that a service hosts it, or that a piece of software is based on it. Writing about Konzilo, teaching it, reviewing it and comparing it with something else are the same.
- **Calling a release by its name.** A release of Konzilo that Kntnt has published may be shared, installed and run as Konzilo while it is unchanged.

Both hold on four conditions:

1. The name is written as text and spelled Konzilo. A logo takes permission.
2. Your own name comes first and stands out more. *Example Hosting, for Konzilo sites* is fine; *Konzilo Hosting* is not.
3. Nothing suggests that Kntnt makes, runs, approves or stands behind what you offer. Where it could be taken that way, say that it does not.
4. What you say is true of what you offer. If it runs or contains a changed Konzilo, say *based on Konzilo*.

## What needs permission

Kntnt's written permission is needed for:

- Konzilo, or a name easily taken for it, in the name of a company, a product, a service, a domain or an account;
- a logo of Konzilo's;
- a word that claims a standing with Kntnt, such as *official*, *certified*, *authorised*, *approved* or *partner*;
- calling a changed version Konzilo;
- goods that carry the name or a logo.

## Changed versions

The license lets anyone change Konzilo and share the result. The name does not follow. A changed version takes a name of its own, one that cannot be taken for Konzilo, and may say that it is based on Konzilo.

A version is changed when its code is. These are no changes:

- setting Konzilo up through its settings;
- building a site on it, with the site's own description, design, components and content.

The name inside the code may stay. A changed version need not rename files, commands, packages or addresses such as `/_konzilo/`, since sites and tools rely on them. This policy is about what people are told they get.

## Hosting Konzilo for others

Anyone may host Konzilo for others. The license allows it, and so does this policy. What the policy decides is what the service may be called.

**Under your own name,** nothing more is needed. Say that the service hosts Konzilo, on the four conditions above.

**Under Konzilo's name,** with the name in the service's name, with a logo, or with a word such as *official* or *certified*, an agreement with Kntnt is needed. It makes the host answer for what the name promises, at least this:

- it runs Kntnt's releases unchanged, and takes a release that fixes a security flaw without delay;
- Konzilo's own acceptance tests pass on its servers;
- a customer can leave: the host hands over the site as its archive when asked, and the archive runs elsewhere;
- no customer can reach another's site or data.

Kntnt withdraws a permission when its conditions are no longer met.

## What the law allows

Nothing here takes away a use of the name that the law allows without its owner's permission, such as naming Konzilo to say truthfully what a product or a service is for.

## Asking, and reporting misuse

To ask for permission, or to report a use that goes against this policy, open an issue in Konzilo's repository, `Kntnt/konzilo` on GitHub. Only an answer in writing from Kntnt is a permission.

## Changes

Kntnt may change this policy. The policy in force is this file on the repository's main branch. A permission already given holds on its own terms.
