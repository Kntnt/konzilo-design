# Konzilo's contributor agreement

Version 1, 10 November 2026

This agreement is between you and Kntnt Sweden AB, of Gothenburg, Sweden, called Kntnt here, which develops Konzilo. It covers what you contribute to Konzilo to be made part of it.

You keep the copyright in what you contribute. You give Kntnt the rights it needs to release your contribution with Konzilo, and to stay free to license Konzilo, with your contribution in it, on other terms than those it has today.

[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) says when this agreement is needed and how you agree to it. Stand-alone work, which a site adds to Konzilo from outside, needs no agreement.

## 1. Words

**You** are the person who agrees to this. Where someone else holds rights in what you contribute, section 4 says what you assure about them.

**Konzilo** is the software and the documents that Kntnt develops under that name, in the repository `Kntnt/konzilo` and in any other repository that points to this agreement.

**A Contribution** is any work of authorship that you send to Kntnt to be made part of Konzilo: code, text, images, data or anything else, new or a change to what is there, in a pull request, a patch, an issue or any other way. What you mark in writing as *not a contribution* when you send it is none.

## 2. Your copyright, and the license you give

You keep the copyright in your Contributions, and may use them as you like.

You give Kntnt, and whoever takes over Kntnt's rights in Konzilo, a license to each of your Contributions that is perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge and of royalties, and irrevocable. Under it Kntnt may:

- reproduce the Contribution, change it, and make other works from it;
- show and perform it publicly, and distribute it, in source or in any other form;
- license it to others, who may license it on in turn, on any terms.

Those terms may be the license Konzilo has at the time, with or without its site exception, another free or open-source license, or terms that are neither.

As far as the law allows, you agree not to hold your moral rights against Kntnt, or against those who have a Contribution from Kntnt, for a use that this agreement allows: changing the Contribution, building it into other works, and releasing it on other terms.

## 3. Patents

You give Kntnt, and everyone who receives Konzilo from Kntnt or through it, a patent license that is perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge and of royalties, and irrevocable except as this section says. Under it they may make, have made, use, offer for sale, sell, import and otherwise transfer your Contributions and Konzilo with them.

The license covers the patent claims that you can license and that your Contribution infringes, alone or together with Konzilo as it was when you sent the Contribution.

If anyone brings a patent action, a cross-claim or counterclaim included, alleging that your Contribution, or Konzilo with it, infringes a patent, the patent licenses that this agreement gives that party for that Contribution end on the day the action is brought.

## 4. What you assure

- You have the right to give these licenses. If your employer, or anyone else, has rights in what you make, they have allowed you to contribute it on these terms, or have given up those rights for it.
- Each Contribution is your own work. Where a part of it is someone else's, you say so when you send it, and say whose it is and under which license or other terms it comes, as far as you know.
- You tell Kntnt if you learn that any of this no longer holds.

## 5. What neither side owes

You need not support your Contributions. You give them as they are, with no warranty of any kind beyond what section 4 assures.

Kntnt need not use a Contribution, and owes you nothing for one.

## 6. How it begins, how long it holds, and the law

You agree by adding your line to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` as `CONTRIBUTING.md` describes, or in another way that Kntnt accepts in writing.

The agreement covers every Contribution you send from the day you agree. You may end it for the future by telling Kntnt in writing. The licenses already given, for the Contributions you sent before that, stay.

A later version of this agreement covers what you send after you have agreed to that version.

Swedish law governs this agreement.
